about 4 px from the retail ink. Load the real Lolita.ttf advances instead:

- crates/wolf-game/src/fontmetrics.rs: FontMetrics parses the TTF with
  ttf-parser, caches per-character em advances, falls back to choice::advance_at

- Game gains font_metrics + set_font_metrics; Game::text_quads routes through
  textpic::layout_with / measure_with when a font is installed.
- CLI install_font_metrics feeds capture/dump/play from the same find_font bytes
  the renderer already shapes with.
