  150 text picture out into style runs. \f[n] sets the run font size,
  anchor bits 12..14 are top-left/centre/bottom-left/top-right/bottom-

  palette, \E adds the outline, and \v/\cself/\cdb/\udb expand.

  screen runs are culled with picture::on_screen.

  default-size item per picture.
