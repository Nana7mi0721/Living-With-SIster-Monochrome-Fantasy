M3: draw the map tile layer and inject one-frame key presses

Game now keeps the current map's TileMap, rebuilt on every start_pages()
from the decoded TileSetData. tile_quads() emits the tile layer first;
capture and play then append the picture layer, matching WOLF's draw
order (tiles underneath every 150 picture).

SampleMapA: 64x36 cells, 3 layers, 27,648 quads covering the whole
1024x576 screen. The opening room is unchanged because its full-screen
bedroom picture covers the tiles.

Also adds --key-frame N:CODE (repeatable) to capture, mapping WOLF key
codes (10 decide, 11 cancel, 12 sub, 2/4/6/8 directions) onto the host
bit set. This is the input path the H-scene 123 polls need; mouse
clicks cannot satisfy them.

Atlas warnings are now folded into Game diagnostics in both capture and
play, so a full or oversized page is reported instead of dropping art.
