fix(vm): persist common-event self variables across invocations (M13)

The VM started every common-event invocation from an all-zero self bank and only
wrote CSelf to Part3 at save time, never restoring it, so per-event state such as
CE251's daily countdown (self51/self65) and CE452's DLC chapter counter (self10)
reset on every invocation. Mainline stage28/29 and the DLC chapters were therefore
unreachable in organic play.

- wolf-vm/exec.rs: add Vm.common_self (EventId -> SelfVars) and common_self_dirty;
  seed a persistent common event's bank on start, fold it back on reap/stop and
  mark dirty; add start_event_fresh for synthetic drivers that must not persist.
- wolf-game/savegame.rs: restore Part3 CSelf0..99 / CSelf5..9 per event on load
  without marking anything dirty (load->save must stay byte-identical).
- wolf-game/game.rs: save_to writes dirty union still-live common banks to Part3
  and clears the dirty set after writing.
- wolf-game/battle_wiring.rs: synthetic CE241/CE242 drivers use start_event_fresh.
- Adapt M7/M8/M9/M11/M12 tests whose expectations encoded the pre-fix zero-self
  replay (verified against SaveData08 Part2/Part3/Part5); visual harness pins
  WOLF_GAME_DIR so subprocess keeps its fonts and h_overlay is re-pinned to the
  save-faithful frame (Part5: pose_09 / word_effect62 opacity 0).

byte-identical; wolf-cli verify PASS 70/0; natural mainline start_day91 drives
