Two real format corrections found by running real code:

* 121 operator 0x6000 (1101 sites) is a random range rand(min,max), not a
  right shift. Operands are intervals (-20000..20000, 1..10, 0..v, 0..99);
  shifting or dividing them is meaningless, and weighted random picks are the
  idiomatic WOLF pattern. Implemented in rng.rs (WolfRng); exact WOLF PRNG
  sequence is still an M2 item.
* compound-assign must mask 0x0F00, not 0x0FFF: 194 instructions carry
  undocumented low bits (0x104, 0x3302, 0x4 ...) that would otherwise degrade
  to plain assignment.
